Questions

Frequently asked questions

What can I expect on a Sunday?

Services are held in Tamil, with interpretation to English available at all locations. Tarneit and Noble Park are bilingual.

Sunday School runs for each service, and is in English.

Can I just show up?

Yes — join us this Sunday at a campus near you. Everyone will feel welcome!

If you like, let us know you're coming or that you visited — fill in our visitor connect form and someone from the church will be in touch.

What denomination are you?

We are a Full Gospel church affiliated with Australian Christian Churches.

Can I invite others?

Yes, please do! We are a Tamil church for families, teens, young and old, new Christians and older Christians — where everyone will feel welcome.
