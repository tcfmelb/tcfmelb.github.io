What to expect on Sunday

Sunday 4:30pm

Tarneit Community Learning Centre, 150 Sunset Views Blvd, Tarneit VIC 3029. Times are subject to change for special occasions. Please get in contact for updates regarding services.

Bilingual · English & Tamil

Services are held in Tamil, with interpretation to English available at all locations. Tarneit and Noble Park are bilingual.

Sunday School in English

Sunday School runs for each service, and is in English. Kids Ministry is for children aged 5–15.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What can I expect on a Sunday?

Our Tarneit service is on Sundays at 4:30pm at Tarneit Community Learning Centre. Tarneit is bilingual — English & Tamil.

Sunday School runs for each service, and is in English.

Can I just show up?

Yes — join us this Sunday at 4:30pm at Tarneit Community Learning Centre, 150 Sunset Views Blvd, Tarneit VIC 3029. Everyone will feel welcome!

If you like, let us know you're coming or that you visited — fill in our visitor connect form and someone from the church will be in touch.

What denomination are you?

We are a Full Gospel church affiliated with Australian Christian Churches.

Can I invite others?

Yes, please do! We are a Tamil church for families, teens, young and old, new Christians and older Christians — where everyone will feel welcome.
